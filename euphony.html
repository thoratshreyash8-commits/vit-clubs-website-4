About Euphony

Euphony is the official music club of VIT Pune. It brings together musicians, singers, and composers to create and celebrate music in all forms.

Activities

  • Solo & Group Singing
  • Instrumental Performances
  • Battle of Bands
  • Music Jams & Workshops

Who Can Join?

Students passionate about singing, instruments, or music production are welcome.
