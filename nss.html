About NSS

National Service Scheme (NSS) aims to develop student personality through community service and social responsibility.

Activities

  • Social Awareness Campaigns
  • Blood Donation Camps
  • Cleanliness Drives
  • Rural Development Programs

Why Join NSS?

NSS helps students develop leadership skills, empathy, teamwork, and a sense of civic duty.
